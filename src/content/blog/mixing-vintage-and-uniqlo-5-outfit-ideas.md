The easiest way to make a UNIQLO outfit look less "off the rack" is to put one worn, secondhand piece into it. The contrast — crisp new basic next to something with real history — is most of what reads as "styled" rather than "just basics." Here are five combinations that work reliably, each built on that single new-plus-old idea.

## 1. Vintage Denim Jacket Over a UNIQLO Tee

A faded, slightly oversized denim jacket over a plain UNIQLO crew-neck tee and straight trousers is close to a default outfit at this point, and it still works because the texture contrast does all the work. Roll the sleeves once and leave the jacket unbuttoned.

![A plain UNIQLO layering top photographed flat on a plain background](/real-photos/55_top_uniqlo_flat.jpg)
*The UNIQLO half of the equation is deliberately plain — a simple layering piece like this is the clean base a worn vintage jacket goes on top of.*

## 2. Vintage Workwear Vest Over the Linen Shirt

We covered this pairing in detail in our [UNIQLO linen shirt guide](/blog/how-to-style-the-uniqlo-linen-shirt) — a cropped or longline secondhand vest in muted indigo or olive layered over the linen shirt is one of the clearest examples of the Tokyo Mix Style approach.

## 3. Vintage Leather Belt with Wide-Leg Pants

UNIQLO's wide-leg trousers (see our [styling guide](/blog/uniqlo-wide-pants-4-ways-to-wear-them)) look noticeably more finished with a worn leather belt instead of a new one. It's a small swap, but leather develops character new accessories don't have yet.

![A pair of relaxed wide-leg trousers photographed flat](/real-photos/22_bottom_vintage_flat.jpg)
*Wide-leg trousers are the canvas for this one. Keep the trouser clean and neutral, and let a worn belt or a vintage top supply the character.*

## 4. Vintage Scarf as the Color Anchor

A printed or textured vintage scarf — silk, wool, or cotton — can anchor an otherwise neutral UNIQLO outfit (think HEATTECH layers in winter) without needing a new statement piece. Knot it loosely rather than precisely; the slightly undone look fits the aesthetic better than a tight, formal knot.

## 5. Vintage Boots with Soft, Relaxed Basics

Pair worn leather boots with soft, oversized UNIQLO knitwear and straight trousers. The structure of the boots balances the relaxed top, and the wear on the leather keeps the whole outfit from looking too uniform.

![A pair of worn leather ankle boots photographed flat](/real-photos/12_shoes_vintage_flat.jpg)
*Structured secondhand boots ground a soft, relaxed outfit. The worn leather is doing the styling — you don't need anything else loud in the look.*

## The General Principle

One vintage piece per outfit is usually enough. The job of the UNIQLO basics is to stay quiet so the single old piece reads as intentional; add a second statement piece and they start competing instead of contrasting.
